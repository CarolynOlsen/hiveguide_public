Dear [Organization/Contact Name],

I am a third-year beekeeper, a volunteer instructor with the UW Milwaukee County Extension beekeeping program, and a professional data scientist. My goal is to support the beekeeping and education community by combining my experience in beekeeping, teaching, and technology.

To that end, I am developing HiveGuide, a digital tool designed to help beekeepers - especially new and hobbyist beekeepers - access reliable, expert information and improve their hive management practices. Among its features, HiveGuide offers smart inspection functionality, voice transcription, a hive sharing system for classroom or family use, and a dashboard with personalized recommendations.

HiveGuide also includes a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) bot that surfaces authoritative beekeeping content in response to user questions. All content from your organization is always cited and presented with a direct link to your original material, ensuring users can access the full resource and proper attribution is maintained.

The hive sharing feature is intended to help beekeeping education programs and instructors use HiveGuide as a teaching aid, making it easier for students to access and discuss trusted resources.

If you are interested, I would be happy to provide test account credentials so you can try HiveGuide and see how your content is presented and cited within the app.

Please let me know if you require any additional information or have specific terms for educational/commercial use. I am committed to respecting your licensing requirements and ensuring your content is used appropriately.

Thank you for your consideration and for your ongoing support of beekeeping education.
